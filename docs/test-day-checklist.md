# Test-day checklist

Most Watson-Glaser tests are taken online, at home, in a window the employer sets. That makes your setup your responsibility. Run through this list before you click start.

## The day before

- [ ] Check the deadline in your invitation email, including the time zone.
- [ ] Check the time limit stated in the invitation or on the test's start page.
- [ ] Confirm any adjustments you asked for, such as extra time, have been applied.
- [ ] Do a short warm-up only. No full practice test the night before.
- [ ] Sleep. Tired readers miss words like "some", "only" and "most".

## One hour before

- [ ] Use a laptop or desktop with a reliable connection, not a phone.
- [ ] Close other tabs and apps. Turn off notifications.
- [ ] Charge the laptop or plug it in.
- [ ] Have paper and a pen for figures and quick diagrams.
- [ ] Have water nearby. Use the bathroom.
- [ ] Tell anyone you live with that you need 45 minutes of quiet.

## In the first minute

- [ ] Read the on-screen instructions fully, even if you have practised. Note the time limit and whether you can go back to earlier questions.
- [ ] Note your clock checkpoints. For 30 minutes: about 10 answered by 7 minutes, 20 by 14, 30 by 21.

## During the test

- [ ] Treat every passage as true. Leave your own knowledge and opinions out.
- [ ] Inference: check the neighbouring label before you answer.
- [ ] Assumptions: run the denial test.
- [ ] Deduction: look for one counterexample. Remember that "some" can mean all.
- [ ] Interpretation: check the group, the measure and the time match. Do the sum.
- [ ] Arguments: "Suppose that is correct." Then judge weight and relevance only.
- [ ] Stuck for a minute? Pick your best answer and move on.
- [ ] Never leave a blank.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Write down which sections felt hardest while it is fresh. If you have another firm's test coming up, that is where to practise.
- [ ] Check whether the firm keeps your score for future applications. Clifford Chance, for example, [saves a passed score for two recruitment cycles](https://jobs.cliffordchance.com/faq-london).

## Last practice before the real one

If you have a day or more, one more timed run is the best use of it: the [free Watson-Glaser practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/watson-glaser?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=watson-glaser).
