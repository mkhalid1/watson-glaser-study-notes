# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when the invitation has already arrived. Both assume you start with the [10-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know your weak sections. Expect about 45 to 90 minutes a day on the 7-day plan.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the [test overview](test-overview.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Note your two weakest sections. |
| 2 | Inference | Read the [inference guide](sections/inference.md). Do all 5 items. For every miss, write one line: which label you chose, the right label, and why. |
| 3 | Assumptions and arguments | Read the [assumptions](sections/recognising-assumptions.md) and [arguments](sections/evaluation-of-arguments.md) guides. Do both item sets. These two areas carry 24 of the 40 items. |
| 4 | Deduction and interpretation | Read the [deduction](sections/deduction.md) and [interpretation](sections/interpretation.md) guides. Learn the deduction "common forms" table until you can write it from memory. |
| 5 | First timed run | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/watson-glaser?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=watson-glaser). Score it by section. Read the [timing guide](timing.md). |
| 6 | Fix the weakest section | Go back to your weakest section's traps list. Redo its items without looking at the answers. Find fresh items for that section and do at least 20. |
| 7 | Second timed run and rest | Take a second full timed test in the morning. Compare section scores with day 5. Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop studying by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

The review is where the score comes from. For each miss, answer three questions:

1. **Which rule did I break?** For example: used outside knowledge, reversed "all", judged truth instead of weight.
2. **What in the text should have stopped me?** Name the word or fact.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "denial test before every assumption".

Keep these in one list. By day 6 you will see the same two or three mistakes repeating, and fixing those is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when the test link has arrived and the deadline is close. Firms often give only a few days: Clifford Chance, for example, [gives four days including weekends](https://jobs.cliffordchance.com/how-we-hire-london). Check your own invitation for the deadline.

### Day 1: learn the five rules

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 20 min | Read the "How the questions look" and "Traps" parts of all five section guides. Skip the rest for now. |
| 3 | 40 min | Do the practice items for your two weakest sections. Read every explanation, including the ones you got right. |
| 4 | 30 min | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/watson-glaser?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=watson-glaser). |
| 5 | 20 min | Review every miss with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: sit the test

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 min | Redo the items you missed yesterday, without the answers. |
| 2 | 10 min | Read the [timing guide](timing.md) and the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 3 | 15 min | Warm up with 10 mixed items you have not seen recently. Stop if you feel tired. |
| 4 | Test | Sit the real test in a quiet room, at a time when you are usually sharp. |

If you only have one evening, do day 1 blocks 1, 2 and 4, then sit the test the next morning.

## What not to do

- **Do not hunt for "real" questions.** Watson-Glaser III draws from an item bank, so recalled questions will not match yours, and using them may break the employer's test rules. Learn the method instead.
- **Do not cram the night before.** A tired reader misses quantity words and opinion markers, which is where most marks go.
- **Do not spend all your time on inference.** It feels hardest, but assumptions and arguments carry 24 of the 40 items.
