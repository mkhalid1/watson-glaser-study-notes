# Watson-Glaser Study Notes

A free, complete study guide for the Watson-Glaser Critical Thinking Appraisal: how the test works, a clear method for each of the five sections, 75 original practice items with full explanations, how scoring really works, and study plans for a week or a weekend. Written for anyone facing the test, with a dedicated guide for UK law applicants sitting it for a training contract or vacation scheme. Facts about the test come only from Pearson TalentLens and employers' own pages, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Take the [free timed Watson-Glaser practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/watson-glaser?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=watson-glaser), or the shorter [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md) in this repo.

## Start here

1. **Find your weak sections.** Take the [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md). It has three items from each section and tells you where to start.
2. **Fix the weakest section first.** Open its guide below, learn the rule and the method, then do its 12 practice items.
3. **Follow a plan.** Use the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan), or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if your test link has already arrived.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Publisher | Pearson TalentLens |
| Questions | 40 |
| Time | 30 to 40 minutes, timed |
| Sections | Inference, Recognising assumptions, Deduction, Interpretation, Evaluation of arguments |
| Score areas | Recognise Assumptions (12 items), Evaluate Arguments (12 items), Draw Conclusions (16 items) |
| Result | Overall percentile plus a percentile for each area, against a norm group the employer chooses |
| Pass mark | None published. Each employer sets its own threshold. |
| Question pool | Watson-Glaser III draws each candidate's questions from an item bank |
| Languages | UK English, US English, French, French Canadian, Dutch, Spanish, Latin American Spanish |

Sources: [Pearson TalentLens](https://www.talentlens.com/watson-glaser.html) and [Pearson's efficacy report](https://www.talentlens.com/content/dam/school/global/Global-Talentlens/uk/CriticalThinking/CTHub/Pearson_Efficacy_Watson-Glaser_2020.pdf), checked 24 November 2026.

## The five sections at a glance

| Section | You decide | Answers | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inference | How likely a statement is, given the facts | True, Probably True, Insufficient Data, Probably False, False | [Inference](docs/sections/inference.md) |
| Recognising assumptions | Whether the speaker takes an idea for granted | Made, Not made | [Assumptions](docs/sections/recognising-assumptions.md) |
| Deduction | Whether a conclusion must follow from the premises | Follows, Does not follow | [Deduction](docs/sections/deduction.md) |
| Interpretation | Whether a conclusion follows beyond reasonable doubt | Follows, Does not follow | [Interpretation](docs/sections/interpretation.md) |
| Evaluation of arguments | Whether an argument is strong or weak | Strong, Weak | [Arguments](docs/sections/evaluation-of-arguments.md) |

Every section guide has the same parts: what it measures, how the questions look, a step-by-step method, the traps that cost points, timing advice, 12 original practice items with explanations, and a score table.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page has links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Answers are hidden under "Answers and explanations". Commit to an answer before you open them.
- **Read every explanation,** including for items you got right. Most explanations say why the nearest wrong answer is wrong, which is where the learning is.
- **Keep a miss log.** For each miss, write the rule you broke. The [study plans](docs/study-plans.md#how-to-review-a-missed-item) show how.
- **Practise timed.** The notes teach the rules. A timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/watson-glaser?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=watson-glaser), teaches the pace.
- **Clone or fork it** if you want to annotate your own copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

## About the practice items

All 75 practice items are original, written for these notes. None are taken from the Watson-Glaser, from Pearson's materials or from any prep site. They follow the published rules of each section so that the method you learn here transfers to the real test.
